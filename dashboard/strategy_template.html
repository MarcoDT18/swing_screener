This page turns the tested edges into a concrete, followable rule set — and then uses Monte Carlo simulation to show what following it would actually feel like: thousands of simulated trading years, each built by resampling the real out-of-sample trades, so the spread of outcomes (not just the average) is visible before any money moves.

Read this before acting on anything above. These simulations inherit every assumption of the backtest — an edge measured in one historical window, modest cost estimates, fills at stops without gaps. Real trading adds slippage, missed entries, and the hardest variable: your own behaviour in a drawdown. The stress panel is closer to reality than the headline panel. Nothing here is financial advice, and no simulation makes the future owe you anything. If you trade this, start at the smallest size until live results and simulation agree.
