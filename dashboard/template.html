Every weekday morning this page re-scans about 700 US and UK stocks and lists the ones showing classic swing-trade patterns. Each row is a candidate worth a closer look — click it to see why it was flagged and a suggested trade plan.

How to read this page

The flags come in three colours. Blue — trending up: stocks starting or riding a climb. Amber — buy the dip: healthy stocks that just pulled back to a level where buyers often return. Purple — unusual activity: heavy buying volume, or a stock that's gone unusually quiet (calm spells often end with a sharp move).

The suggested plan is sized to each stock's own typical daily movement: Buy is the latest price, Stop is where you'd cut the loss if it drops (about 1.5 daily ranges below), and Target is the aim (about 2.5 above) — so a winning trade makes roughly 1.7× what a losing one costs.

Rank is highest when several different flags agree on the same stock and it has also been outperforming the market. More agreement ≈ more interesting.

This is a mechanical screen of yesterday's closing prices, not advice — always look at the chart and the news before trading anything.
